Plans & upgrades

How the subscription works, and what happens at the end.

What happens at the end of my term?

Three options, and they're all yours to pick from: upgrade to the newest model for free, renew and keep the phone you've got, or send it back and stop paying. There's no upgrade fee either way.

Do I own the phone at the end?

No — Talaks owns it the whole time, and there's no buy-out option. That's exactly what keeps your monthly price below what the phone costs to buy. When the term's up you upgrade, renew, or send it back.

Can I keep the phone instead of returning it?

You can keep using it by renewing your plan, but you can't buy it outright. Every phone that comes back to us is refurbished and goes to its next subscriber — that's the part that makes the pricing work.

Is this the same as buying on a payment plan?

Not quite. On a payment plan you're buying the phone, and what's left to pay is a debt you owe. With Talaks you're subscribing to use it. Your total payments stay under the phone's retail price, and once you send it back you're done.

Can I swap to a different phone mid-plan?

When your term ends, swap to any model you like, free. If something better launches and you don't want to wait, message us — we'll work it out, though the early-exit fee on your current plan would still apply.

Can I have more than one phone on subscription?

Yes. Plenty of people run two — one for themselves, one for a partner. Each phone sits on its own plan.

Which terms can I choose?

Every plan runs 12 or 24 months. The longer the term, the lower your monthly price — you'll see both options priced on the device's page before you commit.

Phones & value

The honest answers, including the ones that don't help us sell.

Does a phone get slow after two years?

No, and we'd rather say so. Samsung supports the Galaxy S26 with software updates for seven years. Apple commits to a minimum of five and iPhones typically get five to seven. A two-year-old flagship is roughly a third of the way through its supported life, not at the end of it.

What about the battery?

That's the one thing that genuinely does decline, though slower than it used to. An iPhone 15 or newer is designed to hold 80% of its capacity at around 1,000 full charge cycles — about double the 500 cycles of the iPhone 14 and earlier. In practice a two-year-old phone sits around 85–92% battery health. Most people start thinking about a replacement at year three or four.

So why subscribe instead of buying?

Because of what happens to the money, not the phone. An iPhone 16 lost about 50% of its resale value in its first year; an iPhone 15 is down around 66% after two. If you were going to upgrade at the two-year mark anyway, you're absorbing the steepest part of that drop every single time. Subscribing means we hold the device and take that hit, because it comes back to us and goes out again refurbished.

Is subscribing cheaper than buying?

Over two years, they land close. A 256GB iPhone 17 is $984 across 24 months with us. Buying it at $1,399 and reselling at the two-year mark for roughly $475 puts you around $924 — so buying can be about $60 cheaper, if the resale market holds and you actually go and sell it. What you get with a subscription is no $1,399 up front, no resale to handle, no exposure if values drop, and a free upgrade at the end. We've written this out in full in the Journal.

When should I just buy the phone?

If you keep phones for four or five years, buy one. Depreciation is savage in years one and two and much gentler after, so the longer you hold, the better owning looks. Same if you've got the cash sitting there and you'd rather not have a monthly commitment. A subscription isn't a better deal for someone who was never going to upgrade early.

Applying

What we check, and how long it takes.

Do you run a credit check?

No. Just a quick ID check to confirm you're you — usually under two minutes, and it never touches your credit file.

What do I need to apply?

A photo ID, an Australian delivery address, and a card for the monthly payment. That's it.

Am I guaranteed to be approved?

No — every application is assessed individually, and meeting the minimum requirements doesn't guarantee approval. You'll get a decision the same day.

Damage Cover

Included on every plan. Here's exactly what that means.

What does Damage Cover include?

Accidental damage during normal use — cracked and broken screens, liquid damage, and mechanical or technical faults that aren't your doing. Each repair costs you a fixed service fee and nothing more; we cover the rest. Cover applies up to the value of the device.

How much does it cost?

Nothing extra each month — it's included in the price you see. You only pay when you actually need a repair, and the amount is fixed per device so there are no surprises:

  • iPhone 17, Galaxy S26 — $89 per repair
  • Galaxy S26 Plus — $129 per repair
  • iPhone 18 Pro, Galaxy S26 Ultra — $149 per repair
  • iPhone 18 Pro Max — $169 per repair
  • iPhone Duo — $299 per repair

The fee for your device is also shown on its page before you subscribe.

Is it optional?

No — and that's deliberate. Every plan includes it at no extra cost, so there's no upsell to decline and no way to end up with an uncovered phone by accident.

What isn't covered?

Loss and theft aren't covered on any plan. If your device is lost or stolen you're still responsible for it, and the Non-Return Fee applies — report it to the police and tell us as soon as you can. We also don't cover deliberate damage, or damage from misuse, unauthorised repair or modification.

Is there a limit on repairs?

There's no fixed number. Cover applies up to the value of the device, and each repair carries its service fee. If a device is damaged repeatedly in a way that looks like misuse rather than bad luck, we may decline further repairs under cover — we'd talk to you first.

Will I be charged for scratches when I send it back?

No. Ordinary wear and tear — scuffs, light scratches, normal ageing — is expected over a term and costs you nothing at return.

Delivery & payments

Getting the phone, and paying for it.

How fast is delivery, and what does it cost?

Free, and 3–5 business days once you're approved. Every device ships brand new and sealed.

Is there anything to pay upfront?

No. Nothing to pay to get started — your first monthly payment is the first money that leaves your account.

Can my monthly price change during my term?

No. Your price is fixed for the whole term, and no interest is ever charged.

Is the phone under warranty?

Yes — full manufacturer warranty for the life of your plan.

Ending your plan

Leaving early, returning, and the fees that apply.

Can I cancel early?

Yes. There's a fixed early-exit fee, shown to you before you sign up. It's always a set amount — never the whole remaining balance.

Do I need to keep the box and accessories?

Yes, hang onto the box and anything that came with it, like the cable. You'll need them when you upgrade, renew or return. A small replacement fee applies if something's missing.

What if I don't return the phone?

We'll contact you first and try to sort something out. If it genuinely can't be returned, a Non-Return Fee applies, based on the phone's value at that point less what you've already paid. It's a last resort, not a first step.

What condition does it need to come back in?

Good working order. Everyday marks and wear are expected and never charged.
