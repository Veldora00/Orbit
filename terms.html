These terms are a plain-language summary of how Talaks plans work. The full plan agreement provided at sign-up is the binding, governing document — if anything here conflicts with it, the plan agreement wins.

1. How a Talaks plan works

A Talaks plan is a fixed-term subscription to use a device — it is not a purchase, a lease-to-own arrangement, or a loan. You choose a term of 12, 24, or 36 months at sign-up. Talaks remains the legal owner of the device for the entire term.

Total payments across your term are set at or below the device's retail cash price at the time you sign up. No interest is charged on any plan.

2. Eligibility and identity verification

Approval requires a successful identity verification check. We don't run a credit check, and verification does not appear on your credit file. Meeting our minimum requirements doesn't guarantee approval — every application is assessed individually, and Talaks may decline an application at its discretion.

3. Payments and pricing

Your monthly payment is fixed for the length of your term and shown to you in full, including any Damage Cover you add, before you confirm your plan. Pricing for every device is set from its retail price at the time of your sign-up and does not change part-way through your term.

4. Ownership and returns

Talaks stays the owner of the device for the life of the plan — there is no buy-out or purchase option during or at the end of a term. At the end of your term you can return the device in good working condition within the return window stated in your plan agreement, extend your current plan, or start a new plan on a new device.

5. Damage Cover & fees

Damage Cover reduces what you owe if the device is accidentally damaged during normal use. The exact monthly price is shown on your chosen device's page before you subscribe. It varies two ways: by model, since repair costs differ across devices, and by term — the longer your plan, the lower the monthly cover price.

Cover generally works like this, though the specifics for your device are always shown before you confirm:

  • iPhone plans: unlimited repairs for accidental damage, each subject to a service fee, with theft and loss cover included.
  • Galaxy plans: your first screen replacement is free; a fixed service fee applies to each screen repair after that.

Damage Cover does not cover ordinary wear and tear (which is never charged), and does not cover loss, theft, or damage caused by misuse unless your specific plan states otherwise. Full conditions are set out in your plan agreement.

6. Ending your plan early

You can end your plan before your term is up by paying the early-exit charge disclosed in your plan agreement at sign-up. This is always a fixed, pre-agreed amount — never the sum of your remaining monthly payments.

7. Non-return and lost devices

If your plan ends and the device isn't returned, and no alternative arrangement has been agreed with you, a Non-Return Fee applies. It's calculated from the device's value at that time, less payments you've already made. We'll always try to reach you and sort something out before this is applied — it's a last resort, not a first step.

8. General terms

You must keep the device's original box and any included accessories until your plan ends — they're needed when you return, swap, or upgrade. A small replacement fee may apply if something's missing. Talaks may update these summary terms from time to time; material changes will be communicated to active subscribers. Your full plan agreement, issued at sign-up, remains the governing document for your specific plan.

9. Contact

Questions about these terms or your plan agreement can be sent to info@talaks.com.
