THE MODEL

Why this can cost less than financing one outright.

You're paying for the use of the phone, not buying it. Because Orbit stays the owner, and because every plan is priced at or below the phone's retail price, there's no interest, no finance contract, and nothing owing if you hand it back on time.

  • No purchase optionThe device is always returned at term's end — that's what keeps every plan a straightforward rental, not a loan.
  • Capped exit feeNeed out early? The early-exit charge is fixed and disclosed upfront — never the full remaining balance.
  • Condition-based Damage CoverEveryday wear is expected. Cover kicks in for accidental damage so a cracked screen doesn't become a large bill.
  • Refurbished, not landfilledReturned phones are inspected, refurbished, and given a second lease — which is exactly why the pricing works.

QUESTIONS

Straight answers before you commit.

Do I own the phone at the end of my plan?

No — Orbit remains the owner of the device for the life of the plan. At the end of your term you return it, renew for another term, or upgrade to the newest model. There's no buy-out option, and that's intentional: it's what keeps every plan priced simply, with nothing owing at the end.

Will this affect my credit file?

Approving your plan involves an identity and credit check, which may be recorded on your credit file. This sets the monthly plan amount you're approved for. Meeting our minimum requirements doesn't guarantee approval — every application is assessed individually.

What happens if I want to leave early?

You can end your plan before term's end by paying a fixed early-exit charge, shown to you before you sign up — never the sum of every remaining payment. The exact amount depends on how far into your term you are and is set out in your plan agreement.

What if I don't return the phone?

If a device isn't returned at term's end and we can't reach an agreement to extend or resolve it, a Non-Return Fee applies, reflecting the device's value at that point less what you've already paid. We'll always contact you before this happens — it's a last resort, not a first step.

What if I damage it?

Accidents happen — that's what Damage Cover is for. It's included on every 24- and 36-month plan and available as an add-on for 12-month plans, and it substantially reduces what you'd otherwise pay for accidental screen or body damage. Ordinary wear from daily use is never charged.

Is this the same as buying on a payment plan?

No. A payment plan is financing a purchase — you own the phone once it's paid off, and the balance you owe is a debt. With Orbit, you never own the device, your total payments are capped at or below its retail price, and there's no debt sitting on the phone after you return it.

Plan terms, in plain language

  1. Your plan runs for a fixed term (12, 24, or 36 months) that you choose at sign-up. Orbit remains the legal owner of the device for the entire term.
  2. Total payments across the term are set at or below the device's retail cash price at the time you sign up. No interest is charged.
  3. Approval requires an identity check and a credit and affordability check, which may be recorded with a credit reporting body and used to set your approved monthly plan amount.
  4. You may end your plan early by paying the early-exit charge disclosed in your plan agreement — a fixed amount, not the sum of remaining payments.
  5. At the end of your term, return the device in good working condition within the return window stated in your agreement, extend your plan, or start a new plan on a new device.
  6. A Non-Return Fee applies if the device isn't returned and no alternative arrangement is agreed, calculated using the device's value at that time less payments already made.
  7. Damage Cover, where included or added, reduces your liability for accidental damage. It does not cover loss, theft, or damage from misuse — full conditions are provided in your plan agreement.
  8. These summary terms are for guidance only. Your full plan agreement, provided at sign-up, is the governing document.
